# Texas Killing Fields: design

Apple-style, restrained, documentary tone. The speaker stays untouched in the
centre; one persistent liquid-glass card lives on the left third (x 80-620,
vertically centred) and morphs between five transcript-anchored beats.

- Type: Inter, 600-700 weight, tight tracking, tabular numerals.
- Colour: white text on dark frosted glass; Apple system red (#ff453a) only for
  the words that carry the loss ("missing", "Killing Fields", "serial killer").
- Glass: dark tint, 30px backdrop blur + saturation, hairline white rim, top-left
  specular highlight, a light sweep on each beat change.
- Motion: expo/power ease-outs, blur-to-sharp entrances, rolling odometer digits,
  no bounce, no flashing. Slow 5% push-in on the footage.

Beats (global seconds, anchored to transcript.json):

| # | Anchor words | In | Content |
| - | - | - | - |
| 1 | "Since 1970" | 0.00 | SINCE / 1970 odometer / 1970 to today rule |
| 2 | "up to 30 young women ... missing" | 1.30 | UP TO / 30 odometer / 30-dot grid that empties on "missing" |
| 3 | "Interstate 45 between Houston and Galveston" | 3.30 | I-45 shield, route line Houston to Galveston |
| 4 | "known as the Texas Killing Fields" | 6.55 | KNOWN AS / The Texas / Killing Fields |
| 5 | "police believe ... a single unknown serial killer" | 8.55 | POLICE BELIEVE / 1 / unknown serial killer |
